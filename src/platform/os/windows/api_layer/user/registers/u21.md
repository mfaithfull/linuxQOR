# U21 register entry (verbatim)

- **U21 - the "DC functions" section is not the SDK's DC family, and the SDK's DC family is split across two TUs.**
  Opened 30 Oct 2026 (session 11, `devicecontext.cpp`); **recorded, not fixed** - moving declarations between sections is
  a header-surface reorganisation and the same standing rule as U17/U18 applies. Measured facts:
  - `user32.h:325 //DC functions` declares 8 members, but five of them (`ChangeDisplaySettingsT`,
    `ChangeDisplaySettingsExT`, `EnumDisplayDevicesT`, `EnumDisplaySettingsT`, `EnumDisplaySettingsExT`) are
    display-configuration entry points with no `HDC` in any signature, and only three (`GetDC`, `GetDCEx`, `ReleaseDC`)
    are DC calls.
  - Conversely the SDK declares one DC neighbourhood at `winuser.h:8560-8614` - `WindowFromDC` (`:8563`), `GetDC`
    (`:8569`), `GetDCEx` (`:8575`) in the Desktop-family sub-block `:8443-8580`, then `GetWindowDC` (`:8606`) and
    `ReleaseDC` (`:8612`) in the next (`:8601-8654`) - and this library splits it across two sections and two TUs: three members in `devicecontext.cpp`
    (`:331-333`) and `GetWindowDC`/`WindowFromDC` in the "Painting functions" section (`:274`, `:287`), defined
    `painting.cpp:93`, `:184`. A reader auditing DC-ownership from either TU sees an incomplete family.
  - The folder wraps **no** `CreateDC`/`DeleteDC`, `SaveDC`/`RestoreDC`, `SetDCPenColor`-class or `GetDeviceCaps` member
    in any section (`GetDeviceCaps` is in the `gdi32` layer, not here), so the section name over-promises relative to
    both the SDK block and its own contents.
  Nothing here is a correctness defect - all 8 wrappers bind correctly wherever they are declared.
- **`keyboard.cpp` (session 20, 1 Nov 2026) - the "//Keyboard Input functions" section is not all keyboard either.**
  7 of its 34 declarations are SDK *Window* entry points: `BlockInput` (`winuser.h:14437`), `EnableWindow` (`:7143`),
  `GetActiveWindow` (`:5906`), `GetFocus` (`:5912`), `IsWindowEnabled` (`:7150`), `SetActiveWindow` (`:8508`),
  `SetFocus` (`:5900`). Each is declared **once**, in the keyboard block (`user32.h:72-95`) and **not** in the header's
  own `//Window functions` block (`:128-213`), so the two sections disagree with each other about where the window-focus
  and window-enable calls live, and an audit of either section from its own TU sees a wrong family. Conversely the
  keyboard half is complete: all 34 declarations of the section are defined in this one TU. Recorded, not moved (the
  standing U17/U18/U21 rule: moving a declaration is a header-surface change). Nothing here is a correctness defect -
  all 34 wrappers bind correctly after this session's P1 fixes.
- **Third instance, measured 1 Nov 2026 (session 21, `mdi.cpp`): the MDI family is split 4 + 3.** The SDK's MDI block is
  the `#ifndef NOMDI` region, `winuser.h:12074`-`:12261`, and it holds **seven** entry points. `user32.h:494`'s
  `//Multi Document Interface functions` section owns four of them (`:495-498`, all defined in `mdi.cpp`); the other
  three - `ArrangeIconicWindows` (`winuser.h:12199`, ordinal `E`), `CascadeWindows` (`:12249`, `24`), `TileWindows`
  (`:12240`, `3AE`) - are declared in the header's `//Window functions` block (`:135`, `:139`, `:208`) and defined in
  `window.cpp` at `:58`, `:86`, `:572`. All seven are live exports, so auditing the MDI surface from `mdi.cpp` alone
  under-states it by three - the keyboard/devicecontext shape again. Recorded, not moved (moving a declaration is a
  header-surface change). One difference worth noting: unlike those two TUs, nothing inside
  `//Multi Document Interface functions` belongs to another family, so the section label and its contents agree; the split
  is only that the family's other three members live elsewhere in the header.

- **Fourth instance, measured 2 Nov 2026 (session 22, `menu.cpp`): the menu family is 36 + 1 + 2 elsewhere, and 2 SDK
  menu entries have no wrapper at all.** `tmp/_tmp_menu_nomenus_s22.ps1` walks the SDK's own region markers:
  - `winuser.h:7432`-`:8101` is the `#ifndef NOMENUS` region and holds **38 distinct entry-point stems**; a **second**
    `#ifndef NOMENUS` region at `:10426`-`:10535` holds exactly one more, `CheckMenuRadioItem` (`:10509`). That is why
    `CheckMenuRadioItem` is the one menu wrapper whose SDK prototype is guarded by `WINVER >= 0x0400` + Desktop-family
    rather than sitting in the NOMENUS neighbourhood (`tmp/_tmp_menu_guardsum_s22.txt`).
  - `user32.h:214 //Menu functions` owns 36 of the first region's stems plus that lone one = 37, and two of its 39
    members pair with SDK declarations **outside** NOMENUS: `IsMenu` (`winuser.h:4492`, under `NOUSER` only) and
    `GetMenuBarInfo` (`:14740`, under `NOWINABLE`).
  - Two SDK menu-region stems have **no declaration anywhere in this library**: `ChangeMenuA/W` (`:7481`, `:7485`) and
    `CalculatePopupWindowPosition` (`:8073`) - grep over `user32.h` and the whole of `src` returns nothing. That is a
    sharper case than U4's "declared but undefined": nothing even names them, so no caller gets a `LNK2019`, it just gets
    a missing part of the surface.
  - And the family's context-help pair is declared in a **third** section, `user32.h:641 //Shell functions` (`:642-643`) -
    the T4 pair. So auditing "what does this library expose for menus" from `menu.cpp` alone under-states the SDK family by
    4 (2 undeclared + 2 declared-but-never-defined).
  Recorded, not moved (the standing U17/U18/U21 rule: relocating a declaration is header surface). No correctness defect:
  all 39 wrappers bind correctly.
