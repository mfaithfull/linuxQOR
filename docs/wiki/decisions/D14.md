# D14 - an exported wrapper with no reachable in-tree caller is not a review finding - POLICY (2 Nov 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D14.md` (user decision): open item U15 is **CLOSED
without a fix** and its instances are no longer recorded - a wrapper exported through
`qor_pp_module_interface(QOR_WINAPIUSER)`, defined, linked and callable, but uncalled in
`src`/`examples`/`test`, is published surface; whether the tree exercises it is a consumer-side
question outside the review's scope.

- The protocol change is precise: step 5 still names the consumer one level deep, but unreachable
  wrappers produce no record; reachability stays measured wherever another item's severity turns
  on it (U18, U19); **U4 and T1/T3/T4/T5 stay open** - declared-without-definition is a link-time
  landmine (`LNK2019`), not a reachability question.
- The entry states the closure touched no source, header, or CMake file, so "the review's gate
  (38/38 at `/W4`) is unaffected" - the gate line of
  `src/platform/os/windows/api_layer/user/status.md` is the recorded reproducible leg; it has since
  grown to 54/54, which is scope growth, not violation.
- Stale when a new register starts tallying uncalled wrappers as findings, or an open-item closure
  in `registers/u15.md` is reopened - governance drift, checked by re-reading the register heads.
