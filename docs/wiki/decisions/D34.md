# D34 - T9 step 9h: the 34-member DDE block deleted from `user32.h` - ACTION (9 Nov 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D34.md`: step **9h**, the last step of the D26 tranche queue,
executed as the **delete arm** of the wrap-or-delete decision D26 left open - and every figure was
re-measured this session, not inherited. `user32.h` loses 36 lines (34 members at `:507`-`:540`, the
section banner, one blank), going 855 -> 819; `git diff --stat` reads `user32.h | 36 ---------`,
**1 file changed, 36 deletions(-), 0 insertions**. No new TU, no CMake change - the first T9 step that
adds nothing.

- Why delete, on fresh measurements: **0 of the 34 are declared in `um\WinUser.h`** (the 4 `Dde|DDE`
  hits there are unrelated macros); 27 are declared in `um\ddeml.h` - a library this folder never
  otherwise wraps - and 7 in neither header; **all 34 end `) const;` while `ddeml.h` puts `const` on
  0 of its 100 DDE lines**, so the qualifier is folder-invented and D18's mirror-the-export criterion
  cannot be met for any of the 34; `mxDdeCreateStringHandle` is a mistyped name; and being exported is
  not D18's scope test (38 DDE rows exist on x64 `user32.dll`, 31 of the 34 hit a row, 7 rows carry no
  declaration here - the same 3/7 split `u4.md` measured in October, re-measured not inherited).
- Coverage **moves with the delete, and the move is the exclusion's own evidence** - the only T9 step
  that did: the instrument reads 724 covered / 8 / 286 no-prototype against the published 759 / 8 /
  251, both readings closing at 1,018; the 35 DDE exports that had a declaration join the standing
  exclusion bucket.
- No consumer broke because none existed: 0 `User32::` uses across 3,230 files; the two outside
  mentions are comment banners. The gate is byte-identity: both folder legs SHA-256 identical, and
  the 122-token all-libraries logs identical to each other **and to the 9g post log** (the T2
  precedent) - `user32.h` is out of every other library's reach. Two instrument caveats are recorded
  as instrument bugs, not findings: the nested-`$_` diff clone that printed 166/166, and pwsh `>`
  writing UTF-16 so `Select-String -Path` reads 0 on a dump just written.
- Census after: **600 declaration lines, 600 static, 0 non-static**, 599 bindings - U4's queue
  population is gone and the `) const;` blind spot is gone with it, so the two instruments now agree
  without a caveat. But **T9 CLOSED does not close the ids**: U4/U17/U18 and the rest stay open,
  `u4.md` untouched per protocol 7, and the two closed-TU banners citing block lines (`errorhandling.cpp:53`,
  `processthread.cpp:77`) now carry stale line numbers - write-once banners stay, the shift is recorded
  here.
- Stale when a `dde.cpp` appears, any DDE name returns to the header without a scope decision, or the
  header's `) const;` count moves off 0.
