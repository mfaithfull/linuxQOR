# D15 - the `Library::Call<>` template-argument spacing split (U14) closed as a review item - POLICY (3 Nov 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D15.md` (user decision, session 28): **U14 is CLOSED
without a folder-wide fix and its tally is frozen** - after 28 reviewed TUs both halves (spaced
versus tight angle brackets; explicit versus deducing arguments) are measured to exhaustion
(163/265, 425/3), so finishing it needs a mechanical pass, which is a task, not a per-TU finding.

- Safety proof carried by the entry: spacing never changes codegen - `cl /EP` neutrality showed the
  pre/post multiset identical (154,338 lines, 11 intended lines differing), and the library
  compiled 38/38 at `/W4` with no diagnostics afterwards.
- Protocol change: no more per-TU tallies, nothing appended to `registers/u14.md`; the 3 deducing
  sites hand off to `window.cpp`'s own session; U25 and the `qor_pp_fcontext` check stay live. A
  future spacing pass would open a new numbered task, never reopen U14.
- Stale when a register appends to `u14.md` again or a new U-item re-litigates spacing -
  governance drift read from the register heads; the recorded gate run is the build-side falsifier.
