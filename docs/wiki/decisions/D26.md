# D26 - U4's remaining 86 members land as tranche queue T9; the DDE block stays gated on a scope decision - POLICY (8 Nov 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D26.md` (user decision, 8 Nov 2026): **defer, and land it as
a tranche queue** - the session-11 defer policy's shape, executed in T8's 6a-6g shape of one section,
one new TU, one gate diff per session. No source, header, or CMake file is touched; the decision turns
U4 from an open item into the executable plan **T9** in `registers/tasks.md`.

- The population is one 86, read by two instruments: the static census finds 634 member-declaration
  lines, 548 static, **86 non-static** (the static direction of U4 closed at session 29, so all
  remaining U4 is non-static), and the repo-wide scan finds 52 declared-and-never-defined plus the
  34 `) const;` DDE lines its regex misses by design. They split exactly on the D18 scope line: 52
  in scope across seven header sections, and the 34-member DDE block - declared in `um\ddeml.h`,
  not `um\WinUser.h`, so out of scope twice over.
- What every tranche owes, settled in advance: `static` goes on with the definition in the new TU
  (closing U4's link gap and the U18 shape together); the signature copies the prototype of the
  export it binds (D18); the binding spelling follows D13, and `qor_pp_useswinapiAW` is **mandatory**
  for `SystemParametersInfo`, `GetRawInputDeviceInfo` and `LoadBitmap` because their bare stems are
  `#undef`'d and not exported (G2), so a bare binding caches a NULL `pFunc` for the process lifetime
  (G5). Coverage does not move at 759 of 1,018 for 9a-9g - each step closes a link gap, never a
  coverage gap.
- The three sub-defects inside the DDE row are recorded, not hidden: the 34 `const` qualifiers are
  folder-invented against `ddeml.h`; five stems (`FreeDDElParam` and siblings) are declared in
  neither header; and `mxDdeCreateStringHandle` is a mistyped name for the exports
  `DdeCreateStringHandleA/W`.
- What it does **not** decide: whether 9h's DDE block is wrapped or deleted (out of scope under D18,
  yet 34 unusable non-static members sit on the published class, and D18's direction for U4 is
  completion, not deletion - the question must be asked before 9h executes); and U17's suffix policy
  stays deferred for the same three members T6 held.
- Stale when the T9 row in `registers/tasks.md` is executed out of the 9a-9h order, or a session
  re-derives a per-tranche obligation instead of citing this one.
