# D9 - performance verification needs a per-parse timer + a noise floor - RESOLVED (22 Oct 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D9.md`: judge parse performance only with a timer around
the parse call itself (the committed harness at `test/components/formats/json/main.cpp:284-305`),
and quote a delta only above the noise floor - **13.5% across independent builds of identical
sources**, not the <=0.6% within one binary.

- The baseline it FINALIZED (fixture `test/data/data_2mb.json`, `QORTestJSON` RelWithDebInfo: git-
  HEAD 2110 ms mean vs micro-edited tree 1879 ms mean) lives in run logs deleted from `tmp/` on
  22 Oct 2026 - the entry itself is the surviving record, and older "56 s -> 18 s" and round-16
  figures are declared void/unreproducible.
- Consequence recorded: the three micro-edits (RTTI removal, `#ifndef NDEBUG` guards, power-of-2
  masking) are **performance-neutral within the noise floor** and were KEPT on review grounds,
  with no performance credit claimed; the structural phase is deferred.
- Stale when the harness, fixture, or toolchain changes enough to move the floor - re-measuring
  means rebuilding the whole 21-run procedure, which is exactly why the page carries the D56
  sentinel rather than a judge.
