# D13 - A/W binding spelling for export-bound api_layer wrappers - RULE (29 Oct 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D13.md` (user decision, per-TU at review): bind with
`qor_pp_useswinapiAW(<mod>, Stem)` whenever the SDK has an A/W family; the bare
`qor_pp_useswinapi` only where a single export has no A/W variants.

- The proof is preprocessing, not reading: `cl /EP` on the real TU showed
  `qor_pp_useswinapi(user32, CreateAcceleratorTable)` expands to `GetProcAddress(...,
  "CreateAcceleratorTableW")` - the SDK macro, not the source text, picks the entry. Two failure
  classes confirmed against the live user32 export table: nine `#undef`'d stems resolve to
  unexported literals, and wrapper `T`-suffix spellings stringize literally and are never
  exported.
- Corollary kept as tooling advice: source-grep is not evidence about run-time binding - a binding
  audit must come from `cl /EP` output. The addendum adds the wrapper-name `T`-suffix convention
  with its census and the open item U17.
- Stale when a TU still using a bare spelling for an A/W family is touched (run-time
  `"Missing library function."`), or when U17 closes; `cl /EP` on the TU is the re-check.
