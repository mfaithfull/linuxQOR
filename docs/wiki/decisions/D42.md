# D42 - U21: section/TU grouping by consumer subsystem, not SDK family, is by design - POLICY (11 Nov 2026), CLOSES U21, NO CODE CHANGE

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D42.md` (user, 11 Nov 2026): **TU's do not match SDK families exactly
by design** - the organisation axis is the caller's subsystem, and an SDK feature-macro region feeding several
sections (or one section spanning several regions) is expected shape. U21 closes on the same accepted-as-is arm
as U4, U5, U7, U10, U28; **0 source lines change**, both gates stay at their D38 state.

- **Re-measured, not inherited**: `user32.h` 819 lines, **50** section labels, **54** sources; all seven U21
  instances re-read at their **current** lines - the DC split (3 + 2 across `//DC` and `//Painting`), MDI members
  under `//Window`, `CallMsgFilterT`/`TranslateAcceleratorT`/`IsDialogMessageT` as the `NOMSG` siblings scattered
  across `//Hook`/`//Keyboard Accelerator`/`//Dialog`, and the SDK `WINVER >= 0x0500` region feeding `//Monitor`,
  `//ScrollBar` and `//Menu` at once.
- **Collateral discharged**: `CalculatePopupWindowPosition`, `ChangeMenuT`, `SetMessageQueue` are now declared at
  `user32.h:763`/`:764`/`:809` - and their landing in **Misc/Legacy**, next to nothing menu-shaped, is this
  decision's shape made visible; `ScrollChildren` stays in the 286-name no-prototype bucket (D18's scope line),
  and `GetListBoxInfo`'s hand-off closed with U4 (0 declared-and-undefined repo-wide).
- **Why the fix was never here**: `User32` is module interface (`user32.h:70`), so relocating a declaration is a
  published-surface change chasing every consumer of 600 members - D40 needed 3 call-site edits for a rename; a
  regroup could move link edges in another library. Re-grouping buys no correctness, coverage (**724 of 1,018**)
  or symbol: the folder stays 54 sources / 600 members / 599 bindings.
- Stale when a section label moves or a member's section changes without a recorded decision, or the register's
  frozen instance cites are treated as current line numbers.
