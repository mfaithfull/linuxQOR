# D49 - P4: the four forwarder exports are a table property, both bindings resolve - POLICY (11 Nov 2026), CLOSES P4, NO CODE CHANGE

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D49.md` (user, 11 Nov 2026): **P4 closes as a measured 0-instance
close**. Its four named exports are **forwarders** in the `user32.dll` export table, and the entry itself said
forwarding is transparent to `GetProcAddress` - the close confirms the two halves the entry left open and records
the table fact.

- **Fresh measurement**: `dumpbin /exports user32.dll` gives 1014 address rows + **4 forwarded rows** = 1018
  names; the four read `1668 9F DefDlgProcA (forwarded to NTDLL.NtdllDialogWndProc_A)` style, ordinal+hint,
  **no RVA column** - correcting session 7's frozen prescan claim that they print a real RVA (protocol 7: the
  correction lives here, `registers/prescan.md` gains only its head closure line).
- **Exactly 2 bindings touch them**: `dialog.cpp:53` (`DefDlgProcW` over `User32::DefDlgProcT`) and
  `windowprocedure.cpp:85` (`DefWindowProcW`) - both the W spelling under `qor_pp_unicode` 1, both resolve, and
  the D48 census proved A/W-only forwarded rows cannot be P6-shaped.
- **The pointed-at hazard is discharged**: U6 is resolved (`Call`/`voidCall` build `__stdcall fPtr`, correct for
  all twelve system DLLs including NTDLL targets), and D17's x64-only decision moots the convention question
  outright.
- **The residual value is census literacy**: a parse reading ordinal+RVA sees 1014 of 1018, and the 4 misses are
  exactly these rows - the `gotchas/instrument-flags.md` caveat stays standing.
- Stale when the forwarder rows change (a real RVA appears, an A spelling gains a folder binding) or a new NTDLL
  forwarder joins the table without the census caveat being re-read.
