# D33 - T9 step 9g: the 1 Printing member wrapped in `printing.cpp`, `static`, bound bare - ACTION (9 Nov 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D33.md`: step 9g of the D26 tranche queue, measured fresh,
and its binding spelling is the **opposite** of 9f's. New TU `printing.cpp` defines the one member of
`user32.h:783` (`PrintWindow`), the header gains `static` and nothing else, `CMakeLists.txt`
53 -> 54.

- Binding **1 bare / 0 AW** - the AW spelling is unavailable: the export row IS the bare stem (hint
  2B2; NO_ROW for `PrintWindowA/W/Ex...`), the SDK puts no `#define PrintWindow` switch anywhere, the
  stem is not `#undef`'d, the live probe resolves only the bare spelling, and a compile twin shows
  `&PrintWindowW` failing `C2065` - the compile-side twin of the NO_ROW read.
- Signature (D18) satisfied as declared and compile-checked both ways: positive pointers typed as the
  header and as the export both initialise from `&PrintWindow` (exit 0), a `DWORD`-shaped third
  parameter is rejected `C2440` printing the export's real `BOOL (HWND,HDC,UINT)` shape, and pinning
  `_WIN32_WINNT` to 0x0500 (below the block's double `>= 0x0501` floor) makes the prototype vanish -
  the floor measured, not asserted, under D21's pin.
- Return convention re-read fresh, not inherited: `BOOL` here is a genuine success flag (unlike 9f's
  folding `HBITMAP`), yet U1/U2 still adds no `CheckReturn` (G6's always-on checking would raise a
  continuable issue on every `FALSE`); the flag passes through, the shape `security.cpp`'s wrappers
  stand under. Consumers **0/0** - a raw grep's 4 hits are each measured out (a commented-out
  `WindowController` member, two banner mentions).
- Census: static 599 -> 600, non-static 35 -> 34 (only the DDE block remains non-static), the
  declared-and-undefined scan reaches **0**; coverage stays 759 of 1,018 (link gap). Gate legs:
  standalone 0, folder 54/54 clean, `/EP` 1 bare literal, all-libraries diff 121 -> 122 with the
  9f basename-collision caveat inherited (a `gdi/printing.cpp` already exists; counts, not the log
  set-difference, carry the new TU).
- The step that closes T9's in-scope run: **9h (the 34 DDE members) is blocked on the user's
  wrap-or-delete decision** (out of D18 scope twice over) - the queue has no further open step, and
  the pass's next action is a scope call, not a review action.
