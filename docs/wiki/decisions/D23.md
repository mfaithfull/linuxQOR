# D23 - T5: the IME/WINNLS block wrapped in `ime.cpp`, made `static`, obsolete member kept - ACTION (8 Nov 2026)

The decision, from `docs/agent/decisions/D23.md` (user decision, 8 Nov 2026): **wrap the block and
mark the obsolete member** (of the two honest options the task offered - wrap-and-mark or
don't-wrap-at-all). The unlabelled `user32.h` run between `//Window-Class functions` and
`//Shell functions` is owned by `ime.cpp`: `DisableProcessWindowsGhosting` and `WINNLSEnableIME`
were declared non-static with no definition anywhere (U4 + U18), and the SDK's two WINNLS siblings
`WINNLSGetIMEHotkey` and `WINNLSGetEnableStatus` (`um\WinNls32.h:87`/`:89`) were declared nowhere.

- The pair gain `static` plus definitions bound bare `qor_pp_useswinapi` (single-stem exports
  BB / 3E6, and `WINNLSEnableIME`'s `A`/`W` spellings are MISSING from the export table), and the
  two siblings are added as `static` declarations plus bare-bound definitions in the header's
  order, so `ime.cpp` owns all five IME/WINNLS members. `GetDpiForWindow` stays in `window.cpp`.
- `WINNLSEnableIME` keeps the SDK's obsolete warning as a one-line comment above its declaration
  and **remains on the published interface** - it is not deleted.
- What it settles: for the pair U4's link half and U18's non-static half are gone; the two siblings
  go from undeclared to declared-and-defined. Coverage moves **757 -> 759 of 1,018** (the siblings
  were undeclared real exports, so they add 2; the pair were already covered), census static
  540 -> 544 and bindings 540 -> 544.
- Recorded gate: `ime.cpp` standalone at `/W4` exit 0, folder 46/46 clean, `cl /EP` emits the five
  binding literals (`"SendIMEMessageExW"`, `"DisableProcessWindowsGhosting"`, `"WINNLSGetIMEHotkey"`,
  `"WINNLSEnableIME"`, `"WINNLSGetEnableStatus"`) against module literal "user32", and the shared
  `user32.h` edit is diagnostic-neutral against the 58 external `user32.h` consumers.
- Stale when one of the five definitions leaves `ime.cpp` or the CMake source list, or
  `WINNLSEnableIME` is deleted from the interface without a new user decision.
