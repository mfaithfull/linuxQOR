- **Arm**: even a bare spelling could not fire it here - G1 rewrites `stem -> stemW` before stringizing, the macro pair covers all 7 with **0 `#undef`'d**, and the per-TU `/EP` banners on record emit only the W spellings for these stems. The trap needs the `!UNICODE` arm or an `#undef`'d stem - neither is in this library.
