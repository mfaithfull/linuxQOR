## D42: U21 - the section/TU grouping is by design, not a defect to fix (11 Nov 2026), CLOSES U21, NO CODE CHANGE

Decision (user, 11 Nov 2026): **TU's do not match SDK families exactly by design.** The folder's organisation axis is the
consumer subsystem a caller thinks in (DC, Painting, Keyboard Input, Menu, Message, ScrollBar, Monitor, Misc, Legacy), not
the SDK's feature-macro opt-out regions (`NOMENUS`, `NOMDI`, `NOMSG`, `NOWINMESSAGES`, `NOWINABLE`, `NOCTLMGR`,
`WINVER`/`WINAPI_FAMILY_PARTITION` blocks). An SDK region feeding several sections, or one section holding members from
several SDK regions, is therefore **expected shape**, and U21 closes as accepted-as-is - the same arm U4, U5, U7, U10, U28
closed on. **0 source lines change**, so neither gate is in play (the D36 / D39 / D41 pattern for a close with no edit).

### What the decision settles, item by item

- **(i) a section whose label over-promises its contents** (`//DC` holding five display-configuration members,
  `//Keyboard Input` holding seven SDK Window entry points): **accepted**. The label names the TU's subject, and the TU is
  what a caller browses; the SDK's own neighbourhood boundary is not this library's contract.
- **(ii) a family whose members are declared in more sections/TUs than the one that names it** (MDI 4 + 3, menu 36 + 1 + 2,
  message part-of, monitor 6 of a 45-head SDK region, scroll 12 of 13 exports): **accepted** - an audit-from-one-TU under-count
  is a property of the design, not of the code. Nothing in U21's six instances is a binding, signature, guard or link
  defect; the register's own closing line for each instance says "all N wrappers bind correctly".
- **(iii) the collateral U21's scans recorded as "not even declared"** - `ChangeMenuA/W`, `CalculatePopupWindowPosition`,
  `SetMessageQueue` - is **already discharged and was never U21's to fix**: all three are now declared and defined under
  T8 6f/6g, at `user32.h:763` `CalculatePopupWindowPosition`, `:764` `ChangeMenuT` (one `T` member over
  `qor_pp_useswinapiAW`, D13) in `//Misc functions` `:759`, and `:809` `SetMessageQueue` in `//Legacy functions` `:787`.
  Note the placement: they landed in the **Misc / Legacy** sections, not in the menu/message sections they were found next
  to - which is the shape this decision accepts, now visible in the coverage TUs too.
- **(iv) `ScrollChildren`** stays unwrapped and stays outside this decision's reach: it is exported (`user32.dll` ordinal
  `2283`) but declared **nowhere** in the installed SDK 10.0.26100.0, so it carries no prototype and sits in the folder's
  286-name no-prototype bucket under D18's scope line - a U18 coverage disposition, not a grouping item.
- **(v) `GetListBoxInfo`** - the "declared here, defined nowhere, exported" hand-off U21's monitor scan recorded for the
  `listbox.cpp` session - is closed with U4, not here: T6 added `listbox.cpp` (definition now at `listbox.cpp:98`) and
  `_tmp_decl_no_def_repo_scan.ps1` reads **0** declared-and-undefined repo-wide, so nothing of that shape is left.

### Why the fix was never in this library's hands

- Moving a declaration between sections or TUs is a **published-surface** change: `user32.h:70` is
  `class qor_pp_module_interface(QOR_WINAPIUSER) User32`, so member names and their section/TU placement are module
  interface. D40's rename pass had to follow 3 `ui/` call sites for exactly that reason; a regrouping pass would have to
  follow every consumer of 600 members across `src`/`examples`/`test` and could move a link edge in another library.
- The folder's own convention forbids it independent of cost: U17/U18/U21's standing rule is that relocating a declaration
  is header surface, and the review phase records rather than performs it.
- The grouping is load-bearing only in the direction that already works: each TU keeps its own review banner and each
  member binds its entry point where it stands. Re-grouping buys no correctness, no coverage and no link symbol - coverage
  stays **724 of 1,018**, bindings stay **599**, members stay **600 static / 0 non-static** over **54** sources.

### What D42 does not do

- **No file in `src` changes** - no declaration moves, no section relabel, no section added or deleted, no new TU, no
  `CMakeLists.txt` edit. `user32.h` stays 819 lines / 50 section labels; the folder stays 54 sources / 600 members / 599
  bindings, so the folder gate and the all-libraries gate are not run and both stay at their D38 state (folder exit 0,
  all-libraries exit 2 at zero diff, the baseline's 1 `C1083` at `opengl\wgl.cpp:31` plus 165 warning lines).
- No wrapper is added for `ScrollChildren` (inventing a prototype is a behaviour decision, and D18's scope line excludes
  prototype-less exports).
- `registers/u21.md` carries **only** the head closure line; its instance blocks stay frozen history and are never
  appended to (protocol 7). Their cites stay stale by design - the fresh ones are in this decision.
- The other open leads are untouched: `U26`'s `TCHAR`-width lead, `U27`/`P6`'s `STRICT` question, and U1, U2, U20, U22,
  U24 stay open on their own records.
