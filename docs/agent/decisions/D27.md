## D27: T9 step 9a - wrap the 34 `//String functions` members in a new `strings.cpp` TU and put `static` on all 34 declarations - ACTION (9 Nov 2026)

Step 9a of the D26 tranche queue, executed in D26's own shape (one section, one new TU, one gate
diff per session) and in T8's 6a-6g / T6's `listbox.cpp` shape. Nothing here is re-derived: the
34 members, their `um\WinUser.h` declaration lines, their `user32.dll` ordinals, their SDK guards
and their prototypes were measured once by `_tmp_t9_9a_pre.ps1` into
`_tmp_t9_9a_pre.txt`, and that table is the source of every figure below.

### What landed

  `:13-66`) defines **all 34** members of `user32.h`'s `//String functions` section

### Binding spelling - D13 applied, and the A/W macro is the wrong one here

34 x bare `qor_pp_useswinapi(user32, <literal>)` and **0 x `qor_pp_useswinapiAW`**. This is the
case D13 warns about, seen from the other side: the members already carry the variant suffix
(`CharLowerA`, not the stem `CharLower`), so the binding literal **is** the exported name and
`qor_pp_useswinapiAW` - which pastes `Stem ## W` at `kernel32.h:77` - would bind the member
`CharLowerA` to the export `CharLowerW`. Measured: `_tmp_t9_9a_epcheck3.ps1` on the gate's
`cl /EP` output reads `code_bindings=34`, `code_aw=0`, `code_library_call=34`,
`code_fcontext=34`, `u32_literals=34`, `missing_literals` empty. The sibling layers spell
variant-suffixed wrappers the same way (`kconsole.cpp:624/633` `WriteConsoleA/W`,
`kenviron.cpp:21-48` `GetEnvironmentStringsA/W`, `fileops.cpp:26/35` `SHFileOperationA/W`).

All 34 literals are rows of the loaded `user32.dll` export table, so no binding in this TU can
throw `"Missing library function."` 32 of the 34 are the two halves of an A/W export pair; the
2 that are not are `CharNextExA` (33) and `CharPrevExA` (36) - the table has **no** `CharNextExW`
row and **no** `CharPrevExW` row, so an A/W binding is not merely wrong there, it is unavailable.

### SDK availability and signatures - recorded, not re-derived

All 34 sit under `#ifndef NOUSER` (`um\WinUser.h:64`); the `WINAPI_FAMILY_PARTITION` group differs
by section (`DESKTOP` at `:5388` for the four Oem/CharToOem pairs, `DESKTOP | SYSTEM` at `:5677`
for the Char*/Buff/Next/Prev runs, `:5818` plus `#ifndef NOLANGUAGE` at `:5820` for the eight
`IsChar*`, `DESKTOP` at `:296` for `wvsprintfA/W`, `#ifndef NOAPISET` at `:11173` plus `DESKTOP`
at `:11176` for `LoadStringA/W`), and `CharNextExA` `:5785` / `CharPrevExA` `:5793` additionally
sit inside `#if(WINVER >= 0x0400)` at `:5781`. D21's pin (`kernel32.h:22-30` at `0x0A00`) makes
all 34 prototypes visible, so `user32.h` needs no guard of its own; `NOUSER`/`NOLANGUAGE`/`NOAPISET`
stay unpinned shrink macros, so a consumer that defines one loses the prototype - that is the half
of U16 the pin did not close, and it is recorded here rather than acted on.

D18's rule drove the header edit: the published signature is the prototype of the export the
member binds, parameter names included, and `user32.h` already spells all 34 with the SDK's names
- so the header edit is `static` only. Two behaviours are carried as the SDK states them:
`OemToCharA/W` (`um\WinUser.h:5617`/`:5624`) carry
`__drv_preferredFunction("OemToCharBuff","Does not validate buffer size")`, and these wrappers
pass the destination through unchecked exactly as the SDK does; `LoadStringA/W` take `_In_opt_ HINSTANCE`
and `_Out_writes_to_(cchBufferMax, return + 1)` and return the count read.
