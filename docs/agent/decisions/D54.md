### What the unchecked majority here refuses

`strings`/`keyboard` are the densest TU pair with the sparsest checking (`strings` 34 rows / 10 tokens,
`keyboard` 34/10): the `CharNext`/`CharPrev` family answers with a pointer **into its own argument** ("the return
value is equal to lpCurrentChar" at the string end - a legal answer a NULL-keyed check would flag), and the
`*Buff` variants answer with a character count. `GetWindowLayer`, `GetWindowStyleLayer`, and the `Get*`-shaped
`window.cpp` rows stay token-free on the same reading. Each of the 194 carries its written reason at the site.

### Instrument defects fixed inside this region

- The per-slice report tag must be `[Math]::Floor($skip / $take) + 1`, not `[int]` rounding - `[int](20/3)` rounds to
  7 and wrote `_tmp_u2_misc_fetch_s8.out.txt` for slice 3. Recorded at the misc leaf, corrected there by rename.
- The site classifier must allow **ANSI/Unicode twin headers** (`GetWindowModuleFileNameT` etc.); a bare-stem
  classifier reported 4 rows `NO-DEFINITION` over definitions that exist.
- Decision-entry titles must survive `MatchLongest` - the instrument's `$i+1` offset silently rewrote a `## `
  heading inside a front matter title (`Type: PlanD52`), which is why these entries keep titles on one line.
