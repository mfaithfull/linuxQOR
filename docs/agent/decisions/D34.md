## D34: T9 step 9h - delete the 34-member `//Dynamic Data Exchange functions` block - ACTION (9 Nov 2026)

Step **9h**, the last step of the D26 tranche queue, executed as the **delete** arm of the wrap-or-delete decision D26 left
open on 8 Nov 2026. Nothing here is inherited from D26 or from 9g: every figure below was re-measured this session by
`_tmp_t9_9h_ddecheck.ps1` (the fresh export dump against the 34 header names), `_tmp_t9_9h_sdkcheck.ps1` /
`_tmp_t9_9h_sdknames.ps1` (the SDK declaration site and the consumer scan) and the standing `_tmp_static_census.ps1` /
`_tmp_decl_no_def_repo_scan.ps1` / `_tmp_u18_coverage4.ps1`, with the gate run by `_tmp_t9_9h_folder_pre.bat` /
`_tmp_t9_9h_folder_post.bat` / `_tmp_t9_9h_apiall_pre.bat` / `_tmp_t9_9h_apiall_post.bat` and read by
`_tmp_t9_9h_apialldiff2.ps1`.

### What landed

- `user32.h` loses **36 lines** - the 34 member declarations of the `//Dynamic Data Exchange functions` section (the members
  at `:507`-`:540` before the edit), that section's own `//Dynamic Data Exchange functions` banner at `:506`, and the blank
  line after the section. The header goes **855 -> 819 lines**; `git diff --stat` for the folder reads
  `user32.h | 36 ---------` and **1 file changed, 36 deletions(-), 0 insertions** - the delete is the folder's whole change
  and no other file moves.
- **No new TU and no `CMakeLists.txt` change**: the folder stays **54 sources**, the all-libraries rsp stays **122 tokens**,
  and the binding-line count stays **599**. 9h is the first T9 step that adds nothing - the other seven each landed a TU.

### Why the delete arm, on measurements taken this session

1. **0 of the 34 are declared in `um\WinUser.h`**, so D18's scope addendum (the documented set is the exports `winuser.h`
   declares) puts the whole block outside the review's scope. The 4 lines in `um\WinUser.h` 10.0.26100.0 that match
   `Dde|DDE` are unrelated macros: `SM_CXPADDEDBORDER` `:7359`, `DDL_HIDDEN` `:11609`,
   `SPI_GETKEYBOARDDELAY` `:12457`, `SPI_SETKEYBOARDDELAY` `:12458`.
2. **27 of the 34 are declared in `um\ddeml.h`** (674 lines, 100 DDE lines) - the family belongs to the WinDDF/DDE library,
   which this folder never otherwise wraps - and **7 of the 34 are declared in neither header**: `mxDdeCreateStringHandle`
   (a mistyped name whose exports are `DdeCreateStringHandleA`/`W`), `DdeSetQualityOfService`, `FreeDDElParam`,
   `PackDDElParam`, `ImpersonateDdeClientWindow`, `ReuseDDElParam`, `UnpackDDElParam`.
3. **The 34 spellings copy no SDK prototype.** Every header line ends `) const;`; `ddeml.h` puts `const` on **0** of its
   100 DDE lines, and spells its declarations multi-line with `WINAPI`/`WINDDIDECL` on its own line above the stem
   (`DdeInitializeA(` is one such head). So the qualifier is folder-invented and `mxDdeCreateStringHandle` is a mistyped
   name: D18's criterion - a wrapper's published signature mirrors the export it binds - cannot be met for any of the 34
   without a scope call outside a review action.
4. **Being exported is not D18's scope test, and the delete does not contradict D17.** This session's fresh
   `dumpbin /exports C:\Windows\System32\user32.dll` dump (`_tmp_t9_9h_exports_fresh.txt`) reads **38** DDE rows on the
   supported x64 image; **31 of the 34** header names hit an export row exactly and **3** do not - `mxDdeCreateStringHandle`,
   `DdeInitialize` (its exports are `DdeInitializeA`/`W`) and `DdeQueryString` (its exports are `DdeQueryStringA`/`W`) -
   while **7** export rows carry no declaration here at all: `DdeCreateStringHandleA`/`W`, `DdeGetQualityOfService`,
   `DdeInitializeA`/`W`, `DdeQueryStringA`/`W`. (`_tmp_t9_9h_ddecheck.ps1`; the same 3/7 split `registers/u4.md` measured
   on 30 Oct 2026, so the figure is re-measured rather than inherited.)
5. **Coverage moves with the delete, and the move is the exclusion's own evidence** - unlike 9a-9g, which closed link gaps
   and left coverage alone. `_tmp_u18_coverage4.ps1` post-9h reads **members 601 / export names 1,018 / covered 724 /
   gap SDK-declared 8 / gap no-prototype 286** against the published **759 / 8 / 251** at the 9g close: the **35** DDE
   exports that had a declaration move out of `covered` and into the **no-prototype** bucket - the documented-set exclusion
   bucket this folder already holds 251 names in - and the 8 SDK-visible gaps are untouched and still carry written
   exclusions. Both readings close the instrument exactly: 724 + 8 + 286 = 1,018 and 759 + 8 + 251 = 1,018.
6. **No consumer is broken, and none existed.** `_tmp_t9_9h_sdkcheck.ps1` reads **0** `User32::` uses of any of the 34
   names across `src`/`examples`/`test` (3,230 files); the only 2 mentions of the family outside the header are comment
   banners - `errorhandling.cpp:53` (`DdeGetLastError`, ordinal 8E) and `processthread.cpp:77` (`DdeSetUserHandle`, ordinal
   9C) - and both name the block as U4's, not as a call site. So no caller needed redirecting, no return convention was
   adopted and no `LNK2019` was closed: the block was declared and defined nowhere, and nothing referenced it.

### Gate

is the expected reading because no TU referenced the block. The all-libraries leg is a diff, not an exit code: the standing
**122-token** rsp (no TU added this step) run before the header edit landed and after it landed - **122/122** cpp echoes,
**166/166** diagnostic lines, **0** diagnostics only-in-post and **0** only-in-pre, kinds `C1083` 1 / `C4100` 149 /
`C4995` 16 on both sides, 1 `C1083` at `opengl/wgl.cpp:31` on both, exit **2** on both legs, and the two all-libraries log
SHA-256s are **identical to each other and to the 9g post log** (`42F87200...F4B4A644`) - the T2-close precedent, in which
a byte-identical log is the signal rather than a set difference. `user32.h` stays out of every other library's reach: no
other `api_layer` library includes it, which is why the log does not move at all.

Two instrument caveats this close records:

- The cloned `_tmp_t9_9h_apialldiff.ps1` reads **166/166** for only-in-post / only-in-pre even though the logs are byte-identical:
  its nested `Where-Object { $_ -eq $l }` shadows the outer `$_` and compares against a never-assigned `$l`, so the
  difference is a bug in the instrument, not a finding. `_tmp_t9_9h_apialldiff2.ps1` does the multiset difference with
  explicit variables and reads **0/0** - use that shape.
- A pwsh `>` redirect on this tree writes UTF-16, so `Select-String -Path <dump-file>` reads **0** matches on a dump just
  written (`ordinal` 0, `Dde` 0) while piping `Get-Content <file> | Select-String` reads the 38 rows. A 0 from `-Path` on a
  file you know you just wrote is this bug, not a finding.
