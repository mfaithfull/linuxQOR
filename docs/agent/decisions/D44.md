## D44: U24 - fixed by hand in the gui layer; U24 closes as a single-point exception to the U20 rule (11 Nov 2026), CLOSES U24

Decision (user, 11 Nov 2026): U24's defect - `WindowController::Flash` presenting `FlashWindow`'s previous-active-state
`BOOL` as a "did it work" result - is **fixed by hand in the `winqor_gui` layer**, the register's way-out #3. `WindowController::Flash`
(`src/platform/os/windows/ui/gui/controllers/windowcontroller.cpp:715-724`) now builds a `FLASHWINFO` and calls `User32::FlashWindowEx(&flashInfo)`
with `FLASHW_ALL | FLASHW_TIMERNOFG` (or `FLASHW_STOP` when `invert` is false), which returns a genuine success `BOOL`, so the
method's `bool` finally means "did it work". This is recorded as a **single-point exception**: the U20 rule (a `winqor_gui`/`winqor_gl`
behaviour change is another library's to make, recorded not performed - the arm U22 closed on at D43 and U19 at D41) stays in force
for every other gui-side defect, and U24 is the one the user chose to fix directly in `winqor_gui`. No fact in `registers/u24.md`
is re-measured at this close and the cites below stay exactly as the entry wrote them (protocol 7 - the instance text is frozen history).

### The artefact

- The `Flash` body in `src/platform/os/windows/ui/gui/controllers/windowcontroller.cpp` (`:715-724`) is replaced: a local
  `FLASHWINFO flashInfo` (cbSize = `sizeof(FLASHWINFO)`, hwnd = `WINDOW_HANDLE`, uCount = 1, dwTimeout = 200, dwFlags =
  `invert ? FLASHW_ALL | FLASHW_TIMERNOFG : FLASHW_STOP`) is passed to `User32::FlashWindowEx(&flashInfo)`, whose success `BOOL`
  is returned as the method's `bool`. A short comment block above the member records why (the previous-state return of `FlashWindow`,
  the `FLASHW_TIMERNOFG` stop condition, the `WINVER >= 0x0500` requirement, and the unchecked `FlashWindowEx` wrapper).
- Field names match the installed SDK's `FLASHWINFO` (`cbSize`/`hwnd`/`dwFlags`/`uCount`/`dwTimeout` - the SDK's spelling is `dwTimeout`,
  not `dwDuration`), and the single `PFLASHWINFO` argument matches the wrapper `User32::FlashWindowEx(::PFLASHWINFO)` at `errorhandling.cpp:70`
  / `user32.h:681`. `FlashWindowEx` returns a genuine success flag (`FALSE` on failure), which is what makes the `? true : false` reading
  finally correct - the exact way-out #3 the U24 entry names.

### Why this is an exception, not a change of rule

- U24 is the one gui-side defect the user chose to fix directly rather than only record. The U20 rule stays in force everywhere else;
  D44 does not weaken it, it is a named single exception for this point fix, and the decision records it as such.
- The `winqor_api_user` wrapper is still a faithful pass-through (`errorhandling.cpp:70` forwards one `PFLASHWINFO` verbatim); the change
  is entirely at the gui call site in `winqor_gui`. No wrapper, header, or binding in this library moves.

### Collateral recorded, not fixed

- The `FlashWindowEx` wrapper at `errorhandling.cpp:70` carries no `CheckReturn`, so a `FALSE` from it raises no issue - a **U2 policy gap**
  (the layer deliberately reports no error on these returns), not a TU defect and not part of this close.
- `FLASHWINFO` and `PFLASHWINFO` are declared under `#if(WINVER >= 0x0500)` in `winuser.h`, so the call site compiles only with `WINVER >= 0x0500`
  (the **U16** guard); the gui build's modern default `WINVER` satisfies it.
- The register's "the `FlashWindow` wrapper must not get a `CheckReturn`" consequence is untouched - `errorhandling.cpp:63-68` is not edited,
  so no successful flash of an inactive window gains a spurious continuable issue under G6.

### What D44 does not do

- No `winqor_api_user` source changes: 0 of the folder's 54 sources, `user32.h` stays 819 lines / 600 members / 599 bindings, coverage stays 724 of 1,018.
- No gate is run: the edit is in `ui/gui` (`winqor_gui`), outside the folder's 54-source compile gate and the 122-source all-libraries gate, so both
  stay at their standing post-D38 state (folder exit 0, all-libraries exit 2 at zero diff, the baseline's 1 `C1083` at `opengl\wgl.cpp:31` plus 165 warning lines).
- `registers/u24.md` carries **only** the head closure line; its instance text stays frozen history and is never appended to (protocol 7).
- The other open leads stay untouched: `U26`'s `TCHAR`-width lead, `U27`/`P6`'s `STRICT` question, and U1, U2, U20 remain open on their own records.
