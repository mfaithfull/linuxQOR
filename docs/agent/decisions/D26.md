## D26: U4's remaining declared-but-undefined population (86 members) is landed as a tranche queue T9 - one section per TU per session; the DDE block stays gated on a scope decision - POLICY (8 Nov 2026)

U4 (`registers/u4.md`) is now the folder's largest open population. Re-measured 8 Nov 2026, and every figure below names
its instrument:

- `_tmp_static_census.ps1`: **634** member-declaration lines in `user32.h`, **548 `static`**, **86 non-static**, 547 distinct
  defined member names, **0 defined-but-not-static** and **0 defined-without-a-declaration**. The `static` direction of U4
  is closed (U11's `resource.cpp` pair went in at session 29); the whole remaining U4 is the **non-static** direction.
- `_tmp_decl_no_def_repo_scan.ps1` (searches `src`/`examples`/`test`, not just the folder): **599** parsed declarations /
  **547** defined / **52** declared and never mentioned as `User32::` anywhere in the repo.
- `) const;` count in the header: **34**, all of them in the DDE block - the one shape that scanner misses by design
  (its regex anchors on `);`), so **52 + 34 = 86**, and the two instruments read one population, not two.

The 86 sit in eight header sections, and they split exactly on the D18 scope line:

|---|--:|---|---|
| `//String functions` `:342-375` | 34 | yes | 34 bare (incl. `wvsprintfA` 3F8 / `wvsprintfW` 3F9) |
| `//Raw Input functions` `:543-549` | 7 | yes | 6 bare + `GetRawInputDeviceInfo` A/W (1B7/1B8) |
| `//System Information functions` `:753-756` | 4 | yes | 3 bare + `SystemParametersInfo` A/W (3A8/3AA) |

| `//Dynamic Data Exchange functions` `:507-540` | 34 | **no - 0 hits in `um\WinUser.h`** (declared in `um\ddeml.h`) | 31 bare + `DdeInitialize`/`DdeQueryString` A/W + 1 mistyped |

Three sub-defects sit inside the DDE row and are recorded here rather than hidden: the folder's 34 `) const;` qualifiers are
**folder-invented** - `um\ddeml.h:336` and its siblings declare the same entry points unqualified, so the `const` contradicts
D18's "signature mirrors the export"; `ddeml.h` declares 28 distinct stems against the folder's 34, so `FreeDDElParam`/
`PackDDElParam`/`ReuseDDElParam`/`UnpackDDElParam`/`ImpersonateDdeClientWindow` are declared in neither header; and
`mxDdeCreateStringHandle` (`user32.h:516`) is a mistyped name whose real exports are `DdeCreateStringHandleA/W` (86/87).

The user decision: **defer, and land it as a tranche queue** - the session-11 defer policy's shape, executed in T8's 6a-6g
shape (one section, one new TU, one gate diff per session). No source, header or CMake file is touched by this decision; it
turns U4 from an open item into the executable plan **T9** in `registers/tasks.md`.

| Step | Section | New TU |
|---|---|---|
| 9a | String functions, 34 | `strings.cpp` |
| 9b | Raw Input, 7 | `rawinput.cpp` |
| 9c | System Information, 4 | `systeminfo.cpp` |
| 9d | Button Control, 3 | `buttoncontrol.cpp` |
| 9e | Synchronization, 2 | `synchronization.cpp` |
| 9f | Bitmap (`LoadBitmap`), 1 | `bitmap.cpp` |
| 9g | Printing (`PrintWindow`), 1 | `printing.cpp` |
| 9h | DDE, 34 | `dde.cpp` **or** delete - blocked on a scope decision |

What every tranche owes, settled in advance so a session does not re-derive it: `static` goes on the declaration together
with the definition in the new TU (that step closes both U4's link gap and the U18 non-static shape); the signature copies
the prototype of the export it binds (D18); the binding spelling follows D13, and `qor_pp_useswinapiAW` is **mandatory** for
`SystemParametersInfo`, `GetRawInputDeviceInfo` and `LoadBitmap`, because their bare stems are `#undef`'d at `user32.h:15`/
`:16`/`:21` and are not exported (G2), so a bare binding caches a NULL `pFunc` for the process lifetime (G5). Coverage does
not move at **759 of 1,018** for 9a-9g: the coverage instrument counts declarations and all 52 are already declared, so
each step closes a **link gap** (`LNK2019` at a caller), never a coverage gap - the same arithmetic D24 recorded for T6.
The census moves per step by the section's count (static 548 -> 582 and non-static 86 -> 54 after 9a-9g, bindings 547 ->
599), and `_tmp_decl_no_def_repo_scan.ps1` reads its 52 undefined names down to 0 over the same steps.

What it does **not** decide, and 9h asks before anything executes: whether the DDE block is wrapped or deleted. It is out
of scope twice over - D18's addendum names the **DDE family** as a superseded exclusion, and 0 of its 34 members are
declared in `um\WinUser.h` - yet 34 unusable non-static members sit on the published class, and D18's own direction for
U4 is "completion, not deletion". Wrapping them also raises whether `DdeGetQualityOfService` (export 8F, declared nowhere)
belongs. U17's suffix policy stays open for the same reason it stayed open for T6: `GetRawInputDeviceInfo`, `SystemParametersInfo`
and `LoadBitmap` are 3 of the 11 A/W members that carry no `T` suffix, and the defer policy keeps the eleven until one
folder-wide pass.

Gate for any tranche: the new TU standalone at `/W4` exit 0, then the folder 48/48 (and up) at `/W4` exit 0 with no
diagnostics, `cl /EP` emitting the expected binding literals against the module literal `L"user32"`, and because every
step edits the shared `user32.h`, the all-libraries run as a **diff** against a pre-fix run - the standing baseline is
1 `C1083` at `opengl/wgl.cpp:31`, 165 warning lines, exit **2**, and the source count grows with each new TU.

Record: this D26; the T9 open row in `registers/tasks.md`; the open-task line and the further-session line in `status.md`;
the wholesale rewrite of `preflags.md` naming step 9a as the lead. `registers/u4.md` stays untouched (protocol 7 - the
register already holds the rule and the measured detail; the instances are named here and in each tranche's TU banner).
