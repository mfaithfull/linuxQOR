## D24: T6 - wrap the "//List Box functions" trio in a new `listbox.cpp` TU and make all three `static` - ACTION (8 Nov 2026)

T6 (`registers/tasks.md`) is the whole `//List Box functions` section: `DlgDirList`, `DlgDirSelectEx` and `GetListBoxInfo`
declared non-static at `user32.h:552-554` and defined in no TU, so the published class carries three members that fail
at a caller with `LNK2019` (the U4 declared-but-undefined shape) and stand in the folder's U18 non-static shape. It is
also the T3 shape - a whole section with no owning TU at all. All three entry points are real exports of `user32.dll`:
`DlgDirListA` C1 / `DlgDirListW` C4, `DlgDirSelectExA` C7 / `DlgDirSelectExW` C8 and `GetListBoxInfo` 174 (RVA 0002EFF0),
and no bare spelling of the DlgDir pair exists in the export table.

The user decision: **write the TU - three wrappers in a new `listbox.cpp` - and make all three `static`.** Nothing is
deleted. The two DlgDir members bind `qor_pp_useswinapiAW` for two independent reasons: their bare stems are not exported,
and `user32.h:17-18` `#undef`s both stems, so the SDK's `DlgDirList -> DlgDirListW` (`winuser.h:11596`) and `DlgDirSelectEx
-> DlgDirSelectExW` (`:11638`) macros are erased before the class is declared - a bare binding would stringize a name that
does not exist and cache a NULL `pFunc` for the process lifetime (G2 + G5). `GetListBoxInfo` is a bare single export, so
the bare macro is the D13 spelling there. Each signature copies the prototype of the export it binds (D18): the DlgDir
pair at `winuser.h:11580`/`:11589` and `:11624`/`:11632`, `GetListBoxInfo` at `:14899`. `DlgDirSelectEx`'s header
declaration is also moved from the folder's own parameter names (`hDlg`, `nCount`, `nIDListBox`) to the SDK's (`hwndDlg`,
`chCount`, `idListBox`) - parameter names in a member declaration bind nothing, so that is a readability change on the
published surface, not an ABI change.

What it settles: for the trio U4 is gone (defined, so a caller links) and the U18 non-static shape is gone (static). U17
takes no action - `DlgDirList` and `DlgDirSelectEx` are 2 of the 11 A/W members that carry no `T` suffix, and the session-11
defer policy keeps the eleven until one folder-wide pass; after this TU 6 of the 11 are defined and 5 are still U4. Coverage
does **not** move at 759 of 1,018: all three were already declared, so the coverage instrument already counted them - this
was a link gap, never a coverage gap. The census instrument `_tmp_static_census.ps1` reads 634 member-declaration lines,
548 `static` (was 545) and 86 non-static (was 89), with 0 defined-but-not-static and 0 defined-without-a-declaration; the
folder's binding lines move 544 -> 547 over 47 sources. The coverage instrument `_tmp_u18_coverage4.ps1` keeps its own
member count at 635 and its split at 759 covered / 8 raw / 251 no-prototype, and `_tmp_u18_buckets.ps1` run after it prints
0 for every family except the two all-exclusion rows.

Gate: `listbox.cpp` standalone at `/W4` exit 0, the folder 47/47 at `/W4` exit 0 with no diagnostics, `cl /EP` resolves
this TU's three bindings to the literals `"DlgDirListW"`, `"DlgDirSelectExW"`, `"GetListBoxInfo"` against the module
literal `L"user32"`, and the shared-header `user32.h` edit is diagnostic-neutral - the all-libraries diff against the
standing baseline reads 114 -> 115 cpp echoes, 165 warnings = 165, 166 diagnostics both sides, exit 2 both sides, set-difference
0 new / 0 gone, the only line new in the post log being the `listbox.cpp` echo itself. (A first attempt at that run handed
the response file a path that already ended in `.cpp`, so it compiled `listbox.cpp.cpp` and reported a spurious `C1083`;
the run above is the corrected one, and the corrected run is what the record quotes.)

Record: the new `listbox.cpp` TU and its banner; the `static` and the parameter-name move at `user32.h:552-554`; the
`listbox.cpp` line in `CMakeLists.txt`; this D24; the T6 closed row in `status.md`, its table row and its figures; the T6
resolution note in `registers/tasks.md`; the open-item line and folder figures in `preflags.md`. `registers/u4.md`,
`registers/u17.md` and `registers/u18.md` stay untouched (protocol 7 - closed and frozen registers are never appended to;
the trio's instances are named in this D24 and in the TU banner).
