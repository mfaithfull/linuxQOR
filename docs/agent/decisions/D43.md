## D43: U22 - the note goes where the error is; U22 closes as "cannot be fixed within winqor_api_user" (11 Nov 2026), CLOSES U22

Decision (user, 11 Nov 2026): U22's deliverable is a **note in the source where the error actually is** - a comment block
at the `DeviceContext::Release` member of `src/platform/os/windows/ui/gui/gdiobjects/devicecontext.cpp`, the one member
that issues the `User32::ReleaseDC` call. With that note in place U22 closes as **cannot be fixed within `winqor_api_user`**:
`User32::ReleaseDC` is a faithful pass-through of the SDK entry point, and the HWND/DC pairing is wrong at the `winqor_gui`
callers, so the defect is another library's to change - the standing U20 rule. **No fact in `registers/u22.md` is
re-measured at this close**: the entry's cites are carried into the note exactly as written, and nothing was re-audited,
re-scanned or re-tested.

### The artefact

- One comment block, 25 lines, inserted immediately above `DeviceContext::Release()` in
  `src/platform/os/windows/ui/gui/gdiobjects/devicecontext.cpp` - the member the U22 entry names as the `ReleaseDC`
  call site. It states the route (handle ctor -> `m_hWnd == 0` -> `MustRelease()` -> `~DeviceContext` ->
  `ReleaseDC(NULL, hdc)`), the three `WindowController` sites that take it, the SDK contract that makes the call
  return 0 and leave the DC unreleased, the built `examples/.../mainwindow` caller that makes it reachable, the
  one correct consumer (`PaintSession`, which never sets `MustRelease`), and why the member itself is not the fix.
- The block is comment-only: it changes no token, no declaration, no call, no build input. `winqor_gui`'s compiled
  behaviour is unchanged, so neither the folder gate nor the all-libraries gate is in play for this close (the
  D36 / D39 / D41 / D42 pattern for a close that moves no code).

### Why the fix is not in winqor_api_user's hands

- `User32::ReleaseDC(HWND hWnd, HDC hDC)` (`user32.h` "DC functions" section, defined in `devicecontext.cpp`) forwards
  its arguments verbatim through `Library::Call<int, HWND, HDC>`. It never invents, substitutes or recovers an `HWND`,
  and there is nothing here it could recover one from - the `DeviceContext` that reaches it was built by
  `PerThread::DeviceContextFromHandle` from a bare `HDC`.
- The wrong pairing is made at the call sites: `WindowController::GetADC()` / `GetADC(rgn, flags)` / `GetDC()` set
  `MustRelease()` on an object whose `m_hWnd` the class never set. Making them carry the `HWND` means editing
  `winqor_gui` source and its ownership contract - a behaviour change in another library, which the review phase
  records rather than performs (the U20 rule, the same arm U19 closed on at D41).
- No hook, import-library entry or wrapper in this library can redirect a `ReleaseDC` call that a `winqor_gui`
  translation unit already issues through its own member function.

### What D43 does not do

- No `winqor_api_user` source changes: 0 of the folder's 54 sources, `user32.h` stays 819 lines / 600 members / 599
  bindings, coverage stays 724 of 1,018. No gate is run and both stay at their D38 state (folder exit 0, all-libraries
  exit 2 at zero diff, the baseline's 1 `C1083` at `opengl\wgl.cpp:31` plus 165 warning lines).
- No `winqor_gui` **code** changes: no ctor, factory, destructor, `MustRelease` call or `Release` body is edited, and no
  `CMakeLists.txt` gains or loses a link edge. The only `ui/` edit is the note itself.
- `registers/u22.md` carries **only** the head closure line; its instance text stays frozen history and is never appended
  to (protocol 7). Its cites stay as the opening session wrote them - that is the point of the note, not an oversight.
- The other open leads stay untouched: `U26`'s `TCHAR`-width lead, `U27`/`P6`'s `STRICT` question, and U1, U2, U20,
  U24 remain open on their own records.
